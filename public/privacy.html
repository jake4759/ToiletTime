Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: We collect only what ToiletTime needs to work: your email to sign in, the username, picture and reviews you choose to share publicly, and the map area you're looking at to show nearby places. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how the operator of ToiletTime ("we", "us") handles information in the ToiletTime apps and website (the "Service"). Contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Email address, to sign you in, verify your account and send password-reset codes.
  • Password. It goes straight to our sign-in provider, Supabase, which stores only a secure hash. We never see or store your password.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (or Apple's private relay address) and an account ID from them.

What you choose to share

  • Username, profile picture and map character color. Your username and picture are shown publicly next to your reviews.
  • Reviews, ratings and restrooms you add, including the restroom's location and details. These are public.
  • Reports you make about reviews, and their reason.

Location

  • With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show you on the map, sort restrooms by distance and place new restrooms. Your location stays on your device; we don't store your location or location history.
  • To show nearby businesses and restrooms, the app sends the area of the map you're viewing (not your exact position) to our server, which looks it up in OpenStreetMap.
  • When you search for a business, your search text and the map area are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. When adding a restroom on iPhone, its position may be sent to Apple to look up the street address.

Technical information

  • Our servers receive your IP address and basic request details with each request. We use them briefly for security and to prevent abuse (for example, limiting how often one device can post), and our hosting provider may keep them in short-lived logs.
  • We don't use advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't use cookies for advertising. The website stores your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Service: accounts, sign-in, showing restrooms, reviews and profiles.
  • To send account emails, such as verification and password-reset codes. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To keep the Service safe: preventing spam and abuse, handling reports, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with the law.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • Publicly, as you choose: your username, profile picture, reviews and the restrooms you add are visible to everyone.
  • With service providers that run parts of ToiletTime for us, under their own privacy and security commitments: Supabase (accounts and database), Render (hosting), our email delivery provider (sending codes), Apple and Google (maps, and sign-in if you use it), OpenStreetMap, Overpass and Nominatim (map data and search), CARTO (website map images), and Expo (delivering test versions of the app).
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public or ToiletTime.
  • If ToiletTime changes hands: as part of a merger, sale or transfer, with this policy continuing to apply.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. When you delete it (Profile, then Delete account), we delete your email, username, profile picture and sign-in records. Your reviews stay on ToiletTime without your name, shown as "Former member"; delete them first in My Reviews if you'd like them removed. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time before being overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your username, picture and reviews in the app, and delete any review or your whole account at any time.
  • Turn off location access in your device settings; you can still browse the map.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them as required by law. We won't treat you differently for using them.

7. Children

ToiletTime is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords handled by Supabase, sign-ins stored in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), access checks on every change, and limits against abuse. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Where your information is processed

ToiletTime's servers and service providers are mainly located in the United States. If you use the Service from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we'll let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.
